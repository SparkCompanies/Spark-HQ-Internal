DISPLAY · Bebas Neue

JUST WATCH US

Headlines, big numbers, stat tiles, anywhere you want impact. All caps by design — it has no lowercase. Never use it for body copy; it is unreadable below about 18px.

Install

font-family:'Bebas Neue',Impact,'Haettenschweiler',sans-serif

TEXT · Jost

We are people driven and service focused.

Everything else — body copy, buttons, labels, decks, documents. Weights 300 to 700. Keep body text at 400 and use 600 for emphasis rather than reaching for bold 700.

Install

font-family:'Jost',-apple-system,'Segoe UI',Arial,sans-serif

If a font will not install — on a locked-down machine, or in an email, or on a client's laptop — fall back to Arial rather than letting it land in Times New Roman. Email clients in particular ignore web fonts entirely, which is why the signature below is built in Arial on purpose.

RULES

The short list

Six things that keep the brand consistent. None of them are fussy and all of them get broken regularly.

The registered mark

Spark Companies® is the registered trademark, not “Spark” on its own. The mark belongs on the full name, on every video, site and document. John Joseph Partners is registered too. Spark Talent carries ™, not ®.

Clear space

Leave at least the height of the lightning bolt clear on every side. Nothing — no text, no photo edge, no other logo — comes inside that margin.

The Inc. 5000 badge

Stays in its original, unmodified form — no recolouring, no cropping, no redrawing. Where it appears, it links to the Spark Companies listing on the Inc. site.

Do not touch the logo

No stretching, no rotating, no drop shadows, no gradients, no swapping the gold for another colour, no rebuilding the wordmark in a different typeface. If you need a variant that is not in this hub, ask rather than improvise.

Faith line

Every entity site carries a presence of Christ in some form — a section like Ignite’s, and the footer line “Rooted in our faith in Jesus Christ”.

Portfolio line

Entity sites carry a Powered by Spark Companies® footer line and reference the portfolio in the copy. Every entity site gets a Meet the Team section.
